Why subtracting the mean?

In order to facilitate comparison between sequences that are drawn in different positions by matching their positions.

--------------------------------------------------------

Why not dividing by the standard deviation?

Problematic because the length of the sequences wouldn't matter anymore, however, this is essential for the DTW algorithm because it uses sequence lengths to classify.

--------------------------------------------------------

Why do we do m+1 and n+1 when setting up the DTW matrix?

This is useful for the first iteration, the algorithm works by looking back in time to previous values, which would not work without doing this.

--------------------------------------------------------

Why do we fill the DTW matrix with infinity?

It is required to make sure that when we are running the algorithm and it's comparing the previous neighbours (top, left, and diagonal), the shortest path is always used.

--------------------------------------------------------

Why do we choose k = 1 for the cross validation?

It is faster, better accuracy in smaller datasets, and we would have had to use k=3 (not even) to avoid ties instead, which would take longer.
